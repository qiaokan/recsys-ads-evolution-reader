Glossary

TermMeaning
ALSAlternating least squares: fix one factor matrix, solve the other in closed form, repeat.
ANNApproximate nearest-neighbour search (HNSW, product quantization, ScaNN).
ATTApp Tracking Transparency: Apple's opt-in requirement for cross-app tracking, enforced from iOS 14.5 (April 2021).
AutobiddingThe platform sets per-impression bids to meet an advertiser's budget and target CPA or ROAS.
BPRBayesian personalized ranking: pairwise loss \ln\sigma(\hat x_{ui}-\hat x_{uj}).
CalibrationPredicted probabilities match observed frequencies; required when predictions multiply bids.
CPM / CPC / CPAPrice per 1,000 impressions / per click / per conversion (action).
CTR / CVRClicks per impression / conversions per click.
eCPMExpected revenue per 1,000 impressions; the common ranking currency across pricing types.
FM / FFMFactorization machine / field-aware factorization machine.
FTRL-ProximalFollow-the-regularized-leader online learner with per-coordinate rates and L1 sparsity.
GSP / VCGGeneralized second-price auction / Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism.
HSTUHierarchical sequential transduction unit: Meta's generative-recommender architecture (2024).
IncrementalityConversions caused by the ad, measured by randomized holdout; contrast with attributed conversions.
IPSInverse propensity scoring: reweight logged outcomes by the inverse probability of the logged action or exposure.
logQ correctionSubtract the log sampling probability from logits when training with sampled (in-batch) negatives.
oCPM / oCPCOptimized CPM / CPC: the advertiser targets outcomes, the platform sets impression or click bids with its predictions.
ROASReturn on ad spend: attributed revenue divided by ad spend.
RTBReal-time bidding: per-impression auctions on exchanges, standardized by OpenRTB.
Semantic IDA short tuple of discrete codes for an item, built by quantizing its content embedding, so similar items share prefixes.
SKAdNetworkApple's aggregated, delayed install-attribution API (from iOS 11.3, 2018).
Two-tower modelSeparate user and item networks scored by a dot product, so item vectors can be indexed for ANN retrieval.

Review record

How the content was checked. Each chapter was drafted from the primary literature, then 50 specific dated or numeric claims were sent to two independent fact-checking passes that searched primary sources (papers, company posts, SEC filings, standards documents) without seeing the draft text. A third independent pass re-derived every formula, recomputed every worked example, exercise and chart, and checked 17 further claims; it found no substantive math errors, three wording slips in worked examples (fixed) and the content issues below.
