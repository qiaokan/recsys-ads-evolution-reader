Glossary

Every abbreviation and ad-tech or recommender-systems term used in the reader, alphabetically. Chapters also spell each one out at first use.

TermMeaning
A/B testRandomized online experiment: users split between the current system (A) and a candidate (B).
Ad loadThe share of slots or screen space given to ads rather than organic content.
Ad serverThe system that finally decides which ad fills a publisher's slot.
AdXDoubleClick Ad Exchange, Google's real-time ad exchange after it acquired DoubleClick in 2008.
ALSAlternating least squares: fix one factor matrix, solve the other in closed form, repeat.
ANNApproximate nearest-neighbour search over vectors (HNSW, product quantization, ScaNN).
ATTApp Tracking Transparency: Apple's opt-in requirement for cross-app tracking, enforced from iOS 14.5 (April 2021).
AttributionRules that credit a conversion to an ad, typically one clicked within an attribution window (an allowed time between click and conversion).
AUCArea under the ROC curve: the probability that a random positive is scored above a random negative.
AutobiddingThe platform sets per-impression bids to meet an advertiser's budget and target CPA or ROAS.
Bid landscapeThe distribution of the highest competing bid for an impression; it gives the win probability at each bid.
Bid shadingBidding below value in a first-price auction, by an amount estimated from the competition.
BPRBayesian personalized ranking: pairwise loss \ln\sigma(\hat x_{ui}-\hat x_{uj}).
CalibrationPredicted probabilities match observed frequencies; required when predictions multiply bids.
CALTVCumulative-amount lifetime value: TapTap's model that predicts purchase counts at each in-game price point and sums count × price.
CandidateAn item passed from retrieval to the ranking stage.
CCPACalifornia Consumer Privacy Act, in effect from January 2020.
Censored dataObservations that reveal only a bound: a lost auction says only that the winning price exceeded your bid.
CodebookA learned set of vectors; quantization replaces a vector by the index of its nearest codebook vector.
Cold startRecommending a new item or to a new user with no interaction history.
Conversion valueRevenue the advertiser reports for a conversion (in SKAdNetwork, instead, a 6-bit code the app defines).
ConversionThe action an advertiser ultimately wants after an ad: an install, purchase or sign-up.
CPM / CPC / CPAPrice per 1,000 impressions (cost per mille) / per click / per conversion (cost per action or acquisition).
CreativeThe ad itself: image, video and text assets.
Cross-encoderA model that reads the query and the item together, more accurate but slower than scoring separately encoded vectors.
CTR / CVRClick-through rate, clicks per impression / conversion rate, conversions per click.
D0 / D7 revenueRevenue in the first day or first seven days after an ad click or install; each platform defines the start point.
DCN / DCN-V2Deep & Cross Network: explicit cross layers on top of embeddings (Google, 2017; matrix version 2021).
DIN / DIENDeep Interest Network: attention over a user's behaviours keyed on the candidate ad; Deep Interest Evolution Network adds a recurrent interest model (Alibaba).
DLRMDeep Learning Recommendation Model: Meta's 2019 reference architecture of embeddings, dot-product interactions and MLPs.
DPODirect preference optimization: tuning a generator to prefer outputs a reward model or people rated higher.
DR (doubly robust estimator)Off-policy estimate that combines a reward model with an inverse-propensity correction of its errors.
DSPDemand-side platform: bids in ad exchanges on advertisers' behalf.
DSSMDeep Structured Semantic Model: Microsoft's 2013 two-tower query–document model.
EBREmbedding-based retrieval: candidate retrieval by nearest-neighbour search over learned vectors.
eCPMExpected revenue per 1,000 impressions; the common ranking currency across pricing types.
EMExpectation–maximization: alternate between estimating latent variables and refitting parameters.
Envy-free equilibriumAn auction outcome in which no bidder would rather have another bidder's position at that bidder's price.
ESMMEntire Space Multi-task Model: trains pCVR through pCTR × pCVR over all impressions (Alibaba, 2018).
Examination probabilityThe chance a user looks at a given position at all, under the examination hypothesis of position bias.
F1 (macro-F1)Harmonic mean of precision and recall; macro-F1 averages it over classes.
FAISSFacebook AI Similarity Search: a library for fast vector search on CPUs and GPUs.
Feature crossA feature formed by combining others, such as user country × ad category.
FieldA group of related categorical features, such as all user ids or all ad ids.
First-party dataData collected by the site or app the user is directly using, as opposed to third parties.
First-price / second-price auctionThe winner pays its own bid / the highest competing bid.
Floor (reserve price)The minimum price a seller accepts for an impression or click.
FLOPFloating-point operation; FLOPs is the count.
FM / FFMFactorization machine: a latent vector per feature, pairwise interactions as dot products / field-aware FM, one vector per feature per field.
FTRL-ProximalFollow-the-regularized-leader online learner with per-coordinate rates and L1 sparsity.
Funnel (cascade)Successive stages (retrieval, pre-rank, rank, re-rank) applying more expensive models to fewer items.
GBDTGradient-boosted decision trees.
GDPRGeneral Data Protection Regulation: the EU's personal-data law, enforceable from May 2018.
GEMGenerative Ads Model: Meta's large foundation model for ads ranking (2025).
Generative AI ad creativeAd images, video or text produced or varied by generative models inside ad tools.
Generative recommender (GR)A model that treats a user's action history as a sequence and generates the next item or action (HSTU, OneRec).
GFLOPA billion floating-point operations.
Ghost adsLogging, in an experiment's control group, which ads would have been shown, to compare like with like.
GMVGross merchandise value: total value of goods sold through a marketplace.
GRUGated recurrent unit: a recurrent neural network cell.
GSP / VCGGeneralized second-price auction (pay the bid needed to keep your position) / Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism (pay the value your presence costs others).
Header biddingA publisher queries several exchanges at once before calling its ad server.
HNSWHierarchical navigable small world: a multi-layer proximity graph for approximate nearest-neighbour search.
HoldoutA randomly chosen group of users not shown the ads, used to measure what the ads caused.
HSTUHierarchical sequential transduction units: Meta's generative-recommender architecture (2024).
IABInteractive Advertising Bureau: the industry body that maintains OpenRTB.
IAP / IAAIn-app purchases; in-app advertising (revenue from ads the app itself shows).
IDFAIdentifier for Advertisers: Apple's per-device advertising ID, shared across apps only with ATT consent.
Implicit feedbackBehaviour (plays, clicks, purchases) rather than explicit ratings; it has no explicit negatives.
ImpressionOne display of one item or ad to one user.
In-batch negativesUsing the other items in a training batch as negative examples for each positive.
IncrementalityConversions caused by the ad, measured by randomized holdout; contrast with attributed conversions.
IPSInverse propensity scoring: reweight logged outcomes by the inverse probability of the logged action or exposure.
ITPIntelligent Tracking Prevention: Safari's third-party cookie restrictions, from 2017.
Knowledge distillationTraining a small model to imitate the outputs of a larger one.
LiftThe relative increase in conversions caused by ads: exposed versus holdout.
LinUCB / UCBLinear upper confidence bound: a contextual bandit that adds a confidence width to a ridge-regression estimate.
Liquid welfareTotal value won by bidders, each advertiser's value capped by what it can spend.
LLMLarge language model.
logQ correctionSubtract the log sampling probability from logits when training with sampled (in-batch) negatives.
Long tailThe many items that each receive few interactions.
Lorenz curveCumulative share of a total (here revenue) against cumulative share of the population, with the population sorted.
LTV / pLTVLifetime value: revenue a user brings over a stated horizon; pLTV is a model's prediction of it.
LoRALow-rank adaptation: fine-tuning a large model by training small low-rank weight updates.
MFMatrix factorization: a learned low-dimensional vector per user and per item, rating ≈ dot product.
MFUModel FLOPs utilization: the share of the hardware's peak arithmetic throughput a model actually uses.
MLPMultilayer perceptron: a stack of fully connected layers.
MMoE / PLEMulti-gate Mixture-of-Experts / Progressive Layered Extraction: multi-task architectures that share expert subnetworks.
Modelled conversionsConversions a platform estimates, rather than observes, after privacy changes hid them.
NCFNeural collaborative filtering: an MLP in place of the MF dot product (2017).
nDCGNormalized discounted cumulative gain: a ranking-quality measure that rewards relevant items near the top.
Normalized GiniRanking metric for value models: the Gini coefficient of the model's Lorenz curve divided by that of the perfect ordering.
oCPM / oCPCOptimized CPM / CPC: the advertiser targets conversions, the platform sets impression or click bids with its predictions.
ODMN / MDMEOrder Dependency Monotonic Network and Multi Distribution Multi Experts: Kuaishou's multi-horizon lifetime-value model and its bucketed amount module.
OpenRTBOpen real-time bidding: the IAB's bid request/response protocol.
OPEXOperating expense.
PacingControlling how fast a campaign spends its budget over the day.
PALPosition-bias-aware learning: Huawei's model with separate position and relevance towers (2019).
pCTR / pCVRPredicted click-through rate / predicted conversion rate.
PlacementWhere an ad appears: which app, site, surface or slot.
PNNProduct-based Neural Network: inner/outer products of field embeddings fed to an MLP.
Position biasUsers click higher positions more regardless of relevance.
PostbackApple's delayed, aggregated attribution message to an ad network under SKAdNetwork.
Price of anarchyWorst-case ratio of the best achievable welfare to the welfare at equilibrium.
PropensityThe probability that an item was shown or observed under the policy that produced the logs.
PublisherOwner of the site or app whose ad slots are sold.
QPSQueries (requests) per second.
Quality ScoreGoogle's rating of an ad's expected click rate and relevance, used in ranking and minimum bids.
RAGRetrieval-augmented generation: an LLM conditions its answer on retrieved documents.
RBMRestricted Boltzmann machine.
REINFORCEThe basic policy-gradient algorithm: scale the gradient of an action's log-probability by its reward.
Replay evaluationScoring a new policy only on logged events where it agrees with a randomized logging policy.
Retrieval / rankingCheap selection of a few thousand candidates from the whole corpus / expensive scoring of those candidates.
RMSERoot-mean-square error.
ROASReturn on ad spend: attributed revenue divided by ad spend.
RQ-VAEResidual-quantized variational autoencoder: learns the codebooks that turn a content embedding into a semantic ID.
RTBReal-time bidding: per-impression auctions on exchanges, standardized by OpenRTB.
Sampled softmaxA softmax normalized over a sample of negatives instead of all classes, with a correction for the sampling.
SASRec / BERT4RecSelf-attentive sequential recommendation (causal) / bidirectional masked Transformer for sequences.
ScaNNScalable Nearest Neighbors: Google's vector search with inner-product-aware quantization.
Semantic IDA short tuple of discrete codes for an item, built by quantizing its content embedding, so similar items share prefixes.
Serving pathThe computation run for every request within its latency budget.
SessionThe sequence of a user's actions in one visit.
SGDStochastic gradient descent.
Shadow priceThe Lagrange multiplier of a constraint: the value of relaxing it by one unit (for a budget, one dollar).
SIMSearch-based Interest Model: retrieve relevant behaviours from a lifelong sequence, then attend over them (Alibaba).
SKAdNetworkApple's aggregated, delayed install-attribution API (from iOS 11.3, 2018).
Stay timeTotal time users spend in an app.
SVD / SVD++Singular value decomposition; in Netflix-era usage, a regularized factor model fit on observed ratings (SVD++ adds implicit feedback).
Teacher / studentIn distillation, the large model whose outputs are imitated / the small model that imitates them.
TF-IDFTerm frequency × inverse document frequency: a keyword weighting for content-based filtering.
ThrottlingPacing by entering only a random fraction of eligible auctions.
TIGERTransformer Index for GEnerative Recommenders: generative retrieval over semantic IDs (Google, 2023).
Truthful auctionOne in which bidding your true value is a dominant strategy (second price, VCG).
Tweedie lossLikelihood of the compound Poisson–gamma distribution: a point mass at zero plus a continuous positive part; a built-in objective in gradient-boosting libraries.
Two-tower modelSeparate user and item networks scored by a dot product, so item vectors can be indexed for ANN retrieval.
Uplift modelA model that ranks users by the predicted effect of treatment (an ad) rather than the predicted outcome.
Value maximizerA bidder that maximizes total value won subject to spend constraints, ignoring per-impression surplus.
WhaleOne of the few users who account for a large share of purchase revenue.
Zero-shotUsed on a task without task-specific training examples, from a prompt alone.
ZILNZero-inflated lognormal: zero with probability 1 − p, otherwise lognormal; fitted by one likelihood (Google, 2019).

Review record

How the content was checked. Each chapter was drafted from the primary literature, then 50 specific dated or numeric claims were sent to two independent fact-checking passes that searched primary sources (papers, company posts, SEC filings, standards documents) without seeing the draft text. A third independent pass re-derived every formula, recomputed every worked example, exercise and chart, and checked 17 further claims; it found no substantive math errors, three wording slips in worked examples (fixed) and the content issues below. A fourth pass read the reader as a newcomer would, checking that every term and every symbol is defined at or before its first use in each chapter; it led to the definitions now given in each chapter, the glossary below, a test that fails if any abbreviation is missing from the glossary, and the symbol and correctness fixes in the last rows of this table. Chapter 9 (LLMs) was drafted from about 35 claims first verified against primary sources by a separate research pass; claims found only in press coverage are labelled as press reports or left out, and the chapter then received its own independent review. The seven-day revenue section of chapter 6 was checked against the papers' own text and the platforms' help pages; the last three rows of the table below record what that check changed.
